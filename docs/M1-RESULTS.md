# M1 — Campaign detection results

Generated by `observer/detector.py --eval`. Heuristic detector (no ML), per
CLAUDE.md M1 ("heuristics and clustering first").

## Config
- Combined episode, detect mode (nothing blocked, so campaigns run to completion).
- 178 actors total: 160 benign (Playwright journeys), 16 attack (8 campaigns ×2 runs), plus short-lived noise below the 3-request floor.
- Ground truth = attack-tool user-agent. **The detector never reads the UA or the actor key as a feature** — detection is behavioural only.

## Result (single episode)

| Metric | Result | M1 acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Campaigns detected | **16/16 = 100%** | ≥ 90% |
| Detected within 30 s | **100%** (p50 2.5 s, p90 10.6 s) | within 30 s |
| False positives on benign actors | **0 / 160 = 0.00%** | measured, low |

## The point: catching what CRS missed

The baseline (docs/BASELINE.md) showed CRS blocks 0% of credential stuffing,
IDOR/BOLA and user enumeration — each individual request is unremarkable. M1
catches all of them, and flags them `individually_unremarkable=True` (every
request scored below the CRS threshold):

| Campaign | Evidence | Time-to-detect | Unremarkable to CRS |
|---|---|---|---|
| credential_stuffing | 6 login attempts within 60 s | ~11 s | yes |
| idor_bola | 7 distinct ids on /rest/basket/{id} | ~6.5 s | yes |
| user_enumeration | 4 distinct accounts probed | ~6.8 s | yes |

This is campaign-granularity detection doing exactly what per-request WAF rules
cannot: the whole reason M1 exists.

## Detectors (all behavioural)
- **injection** (sqli/xss/traversal/cmdi/ssrf): ≥2 requests matching a payload-family signature.
- **credential_stuffing**: ≥6 `POST /rest/user/login` within 60 s.
- **user_enumeration**: ≥4 distinct accounts probed at enum endpoints within 60 s.
- **idor_bola**: ≥4 distinct numeric ids walked on one endpoint.
- **scan**: ≥25 distinct endpoints **and** ≥30% 4xx (fan-out alone is normal browsing; an error storm is not).

## Honest limitations (for the viva, and for M1 hardening)
1. **One episode, our own 8 campaigns.** 100% partly reflects that we wrote a detector per attack class we also wrote. Needs held-out variants (different tools, obfuscation, slow-and-low timing) before the number means "general".
2. **Thresholds are hand-set** (e.g. 6 logins/60 s). A patient attacker under the threshold evades — measure that evasion margin in M4.
3. **Single-host range**: actors are separated by the `aegis_vid` nonce standing in for distinct source IPs (see docs/DECISIONS.md). Real actor reconstruction across IP rotation + fingerprint is not yet exercised.
4. **FP measured on 160 benign actors from this episode**, not the full 443k corpus (that corpus predates per-actor separation, so it would merge anonymous users). Re-measure FP on a fresh large benign+separated run.
